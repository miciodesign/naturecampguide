Lido di Dante, spiaggia naturista (Bassona)

Italy, Emilia-RomagnaofficialVerified from sources 2026-10

Romagna's official naturist beach, in a reserve of dunes and pine forest just outside Ravenna. Few services, but clear rules and an ordinance renewed every year.

3.5score /5

Key facts

Statusofficial, signposted
Ground and seabedfine sand with dunes, shallow water
Length400 m (area 1) + area 2 da metà luglio / + area 2 from mid-July / + Bereich 2 ab Mitte Juli
AccessOn foot from the Lido di Dante pine forest along the shore or the paths; crossing the dunes is forbidden. The area starts 50 m south of the forest path.
Servicesmunicipal lifeguard in season, free toilets at Camping Classe; no bar, no litter collection (volunteer cleaning by ANER); parasols forbidden in area 2
Season30 May – 20 September 2026 (ordinance no. 554/2026)

Our scores

  • Nature and scenery 4.5Nature reserve with pine forest and dunes at the Bevano mouth; area 2 opens only after the plovers have nested.
  • Cleanliness 3Driftwood not cleared at the start of the 2025 season and no litter collection: the association does the cleaning (Resto del Carlino 2025-2026).
  • Ease of access 3.5Flat walk through the pine forest, suitable for everyone; distance from parking not documented.
  • Services 2Lifeguard and campsite toilets, nothing else: bring water and shade.
  • Atmosphere and peace 4.5Official with annual ordinance and signage, run by ANER: clear rules, no smoking on the shoreline.
  • Space and crowds 4Quieter than the nearby Romagna beaches (Mondo Balneare 2026); crowds documented only at weekends.

Our verdict

Who it's for

Families and beginners wanting an official beach with a lifeguard, close to the Riviera.

Who should skip it

Anyone wanting a bar, sunbeds and showers at hand.

Practical tip

From mid-July area 2 to the south also opens, bigger and quieter; no parasols there, so arrive early.
