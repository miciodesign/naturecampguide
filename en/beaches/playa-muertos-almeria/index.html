Playa de los Muertos

Spain, Carboneras, AlmeríatoleratedVerified from sources 2025-01

Playa de los Muertos is one of Spain's most beautiful and wild beaches, with unique white pebbles and crystal-blue waters. Located in a pristine natural setting in the Cabo de Gata Natural Park, it is traditionally frequented by naturists. The steep descent keeps it less crowded.
