FKK-Strand Wannsee

Germany, Wannsee, BerlinoofficialVerified from sources 2025-01

The Wannsee is Berlin's quintessential bathing lake, with a history dating back to the early 1900s. The Strandbad Wannsee is the largest outdoor pool in Europe, but in the wild areas around the lake there are traditional FKK areas frequented by Berliners.
