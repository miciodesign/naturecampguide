Guvano (Corniglia)

Italy, Liguria, Cinque TerreunofficialVerified from sources 2026-10

Italy's former naturist icon is now an almost inaccessible cove: tunnel closed, paths collapsed. We list it because many still look for it, not because we recommend it.

Our verdict

Who it's for

Experienced swimmers and kayakers, in a group, with a calm sea.

Who should skip it

Everyone else, and anyone planning to walk there.

Practical tip

If you really want to see it, hire a kayak in Vernazza with a guide: it is the only safe way.
