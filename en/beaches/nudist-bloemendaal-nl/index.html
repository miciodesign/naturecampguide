Nudistenstrand Bloemendaal aan Zee

Netherlands, Bloemendaal aan Zee, Noord-HollandofficialVerified from sources 2025-01

The nudist beach of Bloemendaal is one of the most famous in the Netherlands, located on the dunes of Noord-Holland about 30 km from Amsterdam. The naturist area is in the southern part of the beach, well signposted. Spectacular dunes and lively North Sea.
