Bellevue Strand

DK, Klampenborg, Gentofte, Regione della CapitaletoleratedVerified from sources 2025-01

Bellevue is the most famous beach near Copenhagen, designed by architect Arne Jacobsen in the 1930s. Nudity is generally tolerated in a specific area. The Bauhaus-Nordic design of the beach huts and kiosks is iconic. Very easy to reach by train.
