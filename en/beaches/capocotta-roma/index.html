Capocotta, Oasi Naturista (Roma)

Italy, LazioofficialVerified from sources 2026-10

Capocotta is Rome's naturist beach, inside the Riserva Naturale Statale del Litorale Romano between Ostia and Torvaianica. Officially naturist since 1999, it is frequented by naturists, the LGBTQ+ community and Roman families. The beach kiosk has a troubled history of seizures and renewals, but the beach itself — free and public — is always accessible.

Our verdict

Who it's for

Anyone in Rome wanting a naturist day within driving distance.

Who should skip it

Anyone needing a lifeguard, reliable services or travelling only by public transport.

Practical tip

Before going, check the Oasi's social media to see if the kiosk is open; bring water and a parasol anyway.

How to get there and practical information

Capocotta is at km 33 of the Via Litoranea (SS601), between Ostia Antica and Torvaianica. Access is via an unpaved car park, then a 10–15 minute walk through the dunes. Sandy beach, with water that warms up early in summer. The naturist section is clearly marked; beyond it is a mixed zone. Public transport from Rome: Cotral bus from Termini/EUR to Torvaianica, then on foot or by bike.
