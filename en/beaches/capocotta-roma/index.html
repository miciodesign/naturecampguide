Capocotta, Oasi Naturista (Roma)

Italy, LazioofficialVerified from sources 2026-10

Rome's naturist beach, inside a dune reserve: official since 1999, used by naturists, the LGBTQ+ community and families. The kiosk lurches between extensions and seizures; the beach remains.

Our scores

  • Nature and scenery 4.5Roman Coast reserve: dunes, scrub and no buildings, 25 km from central Rome.
  • Cleanliness 3.5Free beach with no documented organised cleaning; reviews describe a place looked after by its regulars.
  • Ease of access 3Easy by car, but parking is a problem; by public transport bus 07 is unreliable.
  • Services 2.5Everything depends on the kiosk, whose 2026 opening is uncertain; beyond it, no services.
  • Atmosphere and peace 4Established in 1999 by the City of Rome, Italy's first naturist oasis; rules posted at the entrance, quiet and respectful atmosphere (Il Post 2024).
  • Space and crowds 3The stretch is short: on the first sunny weekends it is already full (Open 2023).

Our verdict

Who it's for

Anyone in Rome wanting a naturist day within driving distance.

Who should skip it

Anyone needing a lifeguard, reliable services or travelling only by public transport.

Practical tip

Before going, check the Oasi's social media to see if the kiosk is open; bring water and a parasol anyway.
