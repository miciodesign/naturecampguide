Slapton Sands

GB, Torcross, DevontoleratedVerified from sources 2025-01

Slapton Sands is a long sandbar in South Devon that separates the sea from Slapton Ley lagoon, the largest natural freshwater lake in southwest England. The nudism-tolerated zone is toward the northern part of the beach. Famous for WWII American military operations.
