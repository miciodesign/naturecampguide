Sylt, Buhne 16 (Kampen)

Germany, Sylt, Schleswig-HolsteinofficialVerified from sources 2026-10

Germany's most famous FKK beach, with its most famous bar. Tradition, dunes and white sand; but crowds, scarce parking and blurred nude-clothed boundaries.

3.5score /5

Key facts

Statusofficial, signposted
Ground and seabedvery fine white sand, North Sea
Lengthcirca 3 km misti FKK-tessile / about 3 km mixed / etwa 3 km gemischt
AccessOn foot only, 15 minutes from the 'Sturmhaube' and 'Buhne 16' car parks across the dunes; few, expensive spaces that fill early in summer: better by bike or bus. Visitor tax (Kurabgabe) payable.
ServicesBuhne 16 bar-bistro with kitchen and shop, beach chairs for hire (€12-16 a day), public WC, supervision in sections; showers not documented
Seasonsummer; dogs banned April to October

  • Space and crowds 2.5'Heavily used' according to the municipality; on peak weekends more a party spot than a quiet one.

Our verdict

Who it's for

Anyone wanting to live the Sylt legend and not afraid of company.

Who should skip it

Anyone looking for a quiet, clearly marked nudist beach.

Practical tip

Arrive by bike on a weekday morning; at Whitsun expect a party, not silence.
