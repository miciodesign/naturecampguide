Maspalomas, zona naturista (chioschi 3-7)

Spain, Gran Canaria, Canary IslandstoleratedVerified from sources 2026-10

Europe's winter naturist beach: dunes, year-round sun and hundreds of naturists a day between kiosks 3 and 7. Official by custom, not by decree.

3.5score /5

Key facts

Statustolerated, not signposted
Ground and seabedgolden sand, sometimes strong waves
Lengthcirca 1 km / about 1 km / etwa 1 km
AccessBus 1 or 33 to the Maspalomas lighthouse, then 20 minutes on the sand; or paid parking at the lighthouse (about €7) and 20 minutes; from Playa del Inglés 30 minutes across the dunes. Comfortable shoes, avoid midday.
Serviceslifeguards; kiosks with toilets and showers at both ends, nothing in the central stretch; sunbed and parasol hire, run out early; no natural shade

  • Space and crowds 3Hundreds of naturists every day; enough space but never solitude; arrive by 11 (Tripadvisor).

Our verdict

Who it's for

Couples and singles wanting winter sun; anyone comfortable among crowds.

Who should skip it

Anyone seeking privacy or with difficulty walking on sand.

Practical tip

Settle between kiosks 4 and 6: the most naturist stretch with the least through-traffic. Bring your own parasol.
