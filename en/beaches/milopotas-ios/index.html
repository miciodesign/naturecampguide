Spiaggia di Milopotas

Greece, Ios, CicladiofficialVerified from sources 2025-01

Milopotas is the main beach of Ios, a Cycladic island famous for its nightlife. With its 2 km of golden sand and turquoise waters, it is considered one of the most beautiful in the Aegean Sea. The naturist area is at the eastern end of the beach. The atmosphere is youthful and festive.
