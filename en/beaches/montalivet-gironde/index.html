Montalivet, plage naturiste (CHM)

France, Gironde, MédocofficialVerified from sources 2026-10

The beach where French naturism was born: kilometres of sand in front of the CHM Montalivet. A powerful ocean and lifeguards at only two points.

4score /5

  • Cleanliness 4Large ocean beach, clean according to reviews; no reports to the contrary.

  • Atmosphere and peace 4.5First official naturist beach in mainland France (1966), in front of the world's oldest naturist centre: family naturism, long established.
  • Space and crowds 3Crowded in August (plages.tv, Wikipedia); the length still lets you find space.

Our verdict

Who it's for

Families and anyone wanting 'normal' naturism with a town behind them.

Who should skip it

Inexperienced swimmers: currents are strong and the nude stretch is not fully supervised.

Practical tip

Swim only between the flags in front of the CHM; elsewhere the Médoc ocean is unforgiving.
