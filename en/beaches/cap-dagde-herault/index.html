Plage du Naturisme Cap d'Agde

France, Cap d'Agde, HéraultofficialVerified from sources 2025-01

Cap d'Agde is the world's largest naturist resort, attracting over 40,000 visitors in peak season. The beach stretches over 2 km along the Mediterranean coast with crystal-clear waters. A unique experience combining beach, village and naturist nightlife.
