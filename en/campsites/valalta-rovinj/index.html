Our verdict

Strengths

Cleanliness, pools, sea and complete facilities.

Weaknesses

Price, summer crowds, no pitch booking, no pets.

Who it's for

Families and couples wanting a complete resort near Rovinj.

Who should skip it

Dog owners, anyone wanting somewhere small or a guaranteed pitch.

Practical tip

Arrive in the morning on weekdays to pick your pitch; in June and September it is a different place.
