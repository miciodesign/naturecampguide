Our verdict

Strengths

Price, staff, peace.

Weaknesses

Dated sanitary blocks, no shop, weak mobile signal.

Who it's for

Couples, seniors, tent campers and families on a budget.

Who should skip it

Dog owners, anyone wanting entertainment, a lake or comfort.

Practical tip

Stock up on €1 coins for the showers and shop in Sinsheim before arriving.
