Our verdict

Strengths

Cleanliness, atmosphere, off-season price.

Weaknesses

Path to the beach, Wi-Fi, little for teenagers.

Who it's for

Couples, families with small children, first-timers.

Who should skip it

Anyone with walking difficulties, teenagers wanting entertainment.

Practical tip

Book the lower pitches (10–30) to avoid the climb; June is the best month.
