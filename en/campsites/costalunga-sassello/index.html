Italy, LiguriaVerified from sources 2026-10

Our scores

  • Location and nature 4.5Ligurian hinterland at 400-500 m, views and trails (ACSI surroundings 9.0). Sea about 20 km by road.
  • Facilities 3Pool included, bar with organised dinners every evening, free Wi-Fi; no shop, village 5 km away.

Our verdict

Strengths

Hosts, views, pool.

Weaknesses

Narrow steep access road, no shop, little for teenagers.

Who it's for

Quiet couples and families, hikers, anyone wanting somewhere small.

Who should skip it

Large motorhomes, anyone wanting the sea on foot or entertainment.

Practical tip

Shop before driving up: the last stretch is narrow and the village is 5 km away.
