Our scores

  • Cleanliness and upkeep 2.5The most cited weakness: toilets and common areas criticised on Tripadvisor (2023, 2024) and Campercontact (2023); cleaned once a day.
  • Location and nature 5Terrace 30 m above the sea in the Capo Rizzuto marine reserve, with its own naturist beach: location 4.6/5 on Tripadvisor.
  • Facilities 3.5Restaurant, bar, beach bar, shop, free Wi-Fi, electric shuttle to the beach; no pool.
  • Atmosphere and respect for the rules 3Naturist atmosphere 'unique in Italy' for some; others complain of unfriendly management, unclear entry rules and too many dogs on the beach.
  • Family-friendly 4Baby club 3-6 and mini club 7-12 in July-August, evening entertainment.
  • Beginner-friendly 3FENAIT or INF-FNI card required; one review reports checks on couples at the entrance.
  • Value for money 4€34 flat rate per pitch for 1-2 people in high season (2026 price list), plus electricity €4.50-7.50.

Our verdict

Strengths

Sea and beach, restaurant, quiet.

Weaknesses

Toilet cleanliness, many dogs, criticised management.

Who it's for

Naturists wanting the sea and a private beach who accept a basic site.

Who should skip it

Anyone expecting spotless toilets, a pool or resort comfort.

Practical tip

Go in June or September: warm sea, fewer people and tidier facilities.
