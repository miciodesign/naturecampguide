Our verdict

Strengths

Beach, pools, spacious pine forest, shops and restaurants.

Weaknesses

Paid, slow Wi-Fi, price, reduced facilities off-season.

Who it's for

Families and couples including seniors, cyclists, dog owners.

Who should skip it

Remote workers, anyone seeking strict naturism or with a very large motorhome.

Practical tip

Bring a bike: internal distances run to kilometres. Off-season, ask which facilities are open.
