Koversada Uncovered Naturist (Maistra)

Croatia, IstriaVerified from sources 2026-10

The historic Koversada is no more: since 2025 the naturist part is a fenced section of a textile resort. Excellent pitches and sanitary blocks, but reduced facilities and beach.

Our scores

  • Cleanliness and upkeep 4.5Modern sanitary blocks cleaned several times a day (camping.info 2025-2026; PiNCAMP cleanliness 8.6/10).
  • Location and nature 4Lim bay at Vrsar, rocky coves right on site; the naturist beach is however smaller than it used to be.
  • Facilities 2.5In the naturist section only a food truck and partial Wi-Fi; pools, restaurants, supermarket and kids' club are in the textile section, with a dress code.
  • Atmosphere and respect for the rules 2.5Since 2025 the naturist area is about a quarter of the resort, behind a fence; many regulars feel 'no longer wanted' (PiNCAMP, camping.info 2024-2025).
  • Family-friendly 3Kids' club only in the textile section; large pitches and a dog beach.
  • Beginner-friendly 3.5No card, spacious pitches; but sharing the site with a textile section can confuse first-timers.
  • Value for money 2.5From €75 a night for two in high season (PiNCAMP 2026 comparison price) with reduced facilities: value 5.7/10 on PiNCAMP.

Our verdict

Strengths

Large pitches, new sanitary blocks, dogs allowed.

Weaknesses

Shrunken naturist area, high prices, facilities in the textile part.

Who it's for

Anyone wanting space, new sanitary blocks and travelling with a dog, who accepts the new set-up.

Who should skip it

Naturists attached to the old Koversada, and anyone wanting pool and shop without getting dressed.

Practical tip

Before booking, ask explicitly for a pitch in the 'Uncovered' section near the coast.
