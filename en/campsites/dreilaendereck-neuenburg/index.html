Our scores

  • Cleanliness and upkeep 5Sanitary blocks rated 10/10 on PiNCAMP, the most cited strength.
  • Location and nature 4Over 20 hectares with a swimming lake and sandy beach, in the Rhine plain between Basel and Freiburg.
  • Facilities 4No pool but lake, sauna, whirlpool, summer bistro, bakery, butcher and farmers' market; Wi-Fi paid and criticised.
  • Atmosphere and respect for the rules 4Quiet, many seasonal residents (360 permanent pitches); reception occasionally rated unfriendly (PiNCAMP).
  • Family-friendly 4LiLaLu kids' club, ponies, petting zoo; playground due for renewal according to reviews.
  • Beginner-friendly 4Couples and families without a card; singles only with a valid INF card.
  • Value for money 2.5€48 a night for two with car in high season (2026 price list) plus metered electricity: rated expensive for the facilities (PiNCAMP 5.9/10).

Our verdict

Strengths

Sanitary blocks, lake and wellness, winter opening.

Weaknesses

Price, Wi-Fi, facilities needing renewal.

Who it's for

Families and couples seeking lake, sauna and a base for Basel, Freiburg and Alsace.

Who should skip it

Anyone wanting a pool, a tight budget or reliable Wi-Fi.

Practical tip

In spring and autumn (Saison II) prices drop and the sauna pays off more.
