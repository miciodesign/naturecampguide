Naturist beaches and campsites in Puglia

Puglia is one of the most interesting naturist destinations in southern Italy: clear waters, rocky and sandy coasts, and accommodation ranging from nature reserves to affiliated campsites. The beach of Torre Guaceto, within the state nature reserve of the same name near Brindisi, is protected from fishing and industrial activities: the sea is among the cleanest on the Adriatic coast. The Gargano, meanwhile, is home to historic naturist campsites with direct sea access.
