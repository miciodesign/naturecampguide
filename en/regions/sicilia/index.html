Naturist beaches and campsites in Sicily

Sicily is home to Calamosche, considered one of the most beautiful beaches in Italy: very fine white sand, emerald-coloured waters, within the Vendicari Nature Reserve near Siracusa. Access is on foot (about 20 minutes from the car park), the beach is busy but not overcrowded, and naturism is practised discreetly in the part furthest from the entrance.
