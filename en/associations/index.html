Naturist associations in Europe

The main federations that promote and protect naturism, country by country. Through these organisations you can find affiliated campsites, internationally recognised membership cards and up-to-date information on beaches.

International organisations

International Naturist Federation (INF-FNI)

The world federation of naturism, founded in 1953. It groups national federations from over 30 countries and issues the INF international membership card, recognised by most affiliated campsites and beaches.

Official website ↗

European Naturist Federation (ENF)

The European branch of INF, coordinating the continent's federations and organising events like the Europade, a roving European naturist gathering.

Official website ↗

National federations

🇮🇹 Italy

FENAIT – Federazione Naturista Italiana

The Italian naturist federation, affiliated with INF. Issues the INF card for Italy and maintains the list of affiliated naturist campsites and clubs.

Official website ↗
🇫🇷 France

FFN – Fédération Française de Naturisme

The largest naturist federation in Europe by membership. It manages many naturist campsites in France, especially in the Landes region, and issues the annual FFN card.

Official website ↗
🇩🇪 Germany

DFK – Deutscher Verband für Freikörperkultur

The world's oldest organised nudist movement (1898). Groups German FKK clubs and manages campsites across Germany, issuing the DFK card recognised at INF level.

Official website ↗
🇪🇸 Spain

FEN – Federación Española de Naturismo

The Spanish federation, INF-affiliated. Coordinates naturist clubs and campsites in Spain and promotes official naturist beaches.

Official website ↗
🇭🇷 Croatia

HNS – Hrvatska naturistička savez

The Croatian naturist federation. Croatia has one of the longest naturist traditions in Europe, with numerous FKK campsites and beaches, especially in Istria.

Official website ↗
🇬🇧 GB

British Naturism (BN)

The main British naturism body, founded in 1964. Issues the BN/INF card and promotes naturist beaches, clubs and campsites across the UK.

Official website ↗
🇳🇱 Netherlands

NFN – Nederlandse Federatie van Naturisten

The Dutch federation, with a network of affiliated campsites and clubs in the Netherlands. Issues the INF card and organises national events.

Official website ↗
🇩🇰 DK

DNF – Dansk Naturist Forbund

The Danish naturist federation, INF-affiliated. Manages naturist clubs and facilities in Denmark.

Official website ↗
🇵🇹 Portugal

FPN – Federação Portuguesa de Naturismo

The Portuguese federation, INF-affiliated. Portugal has some of Europe's finest naturist beaches, especially in the Algarve.

Official website ↗
🇬🇷 Greece

HNF – Hellenic Naturist Federation

The Greek naturist federation. Greece has numerous official and tolerated naturist beaches, especially on the islands.

Official website ↗
